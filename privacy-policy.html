Privacy Policy

Align'd Wellness

Effective Date: October 23, 2026 · Last Updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Align'd Wellness ("Align'd," "we," "us," or "our") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have when you use the Align'd Wellness mobile app and this website (together, the "Service").

In short: your cycle and health data stays on your phone unless you sign in to back it up. We have no ads, we don't sell your data, and we don't use third-party analytics or tracking.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Name or display name, and year of birth
  • Height and weight, if you add them to your profile
  • Email address, if you create an account (Sign in with Apple, Google, or email)

Cycle and health information

  • Period dates, flow, and cycle predictions
  • Symptoms, moods, energy, stress and sleep from your daily check-ins and logs
  • Health conditions you choose to share (for example PCOS or endometriosis) and your birth control method
  • Your wellness goals and wellness scores

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health (in the app: Menu → Apple Health), Align'd reads your heart rate, heart rate variability and body temperature to fine-tune your cycle predictions. Align'd only reads this data and never writes to Apple Health. These readings are kept on your phone, and in your account backup if you are signed in. Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties. You can turn access off at any time in the Health app → Profile → Apps → Align'd Wellness. On Android, the same applies to Health Connect.

Community posts

Posts and replies you write in Community are shown to other users under an anonymous handle and stored on our servers. Please don't include anything that identifies you. If someone reports a post, our team reviews it.

Shop orders

When you place an order we collect your name, phone number, delivery address and the items you ordered, so we can deliver it and contact you about it. Payment is cash on delivery — we do not collect card or bank details.

Partner Connect

If you connect a partner, only the categories you choose to share are made available to them. You can change this or disconnect at any time.

Location

Safe Space uses your location only while that screen is open, to show hospitals, police stations, pharmacies and other help near you. Your approximate location is sent to Google Maps to load the map and nearby places. We do not store your location, and the app never tracks your location in the background.

What we don't collect

  • No advertising identifiers, ad networks, or tracking across other apps and websites
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools
  • Messages to the in-app wellness assistant are handled on your phone and are not sent to us or to any AI provider

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To run the app: cycle predictions, daily insights, wellness scores and reminders
  • To back up and restore your data when you sign in
  • To show and moderate Community posts
  • To deliver Shop orders and contact you about them
  • To answer your emails and keep the Service safe
  • To comply with the law

We do not use your health data for advertising, and we do not sell, rent or share your personal or health data with anyone for their own marketing.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

On your phone

Your cycles, daily logs, check-ins and health profile are stored in the app's private storage on your phone, protected by your phone's passcode and built-in device security. If you never sign in, this data does not leave your phone.

Back up & Sync

If you sign in, your profile, cycle and health data is backed up to your Align'd account so you can restore it on another device. Our database is provided by Supabase. Data is sent over an encrypted connection (TLS), encrypted at rest by Supabase, and protected by access rules so that only your account can read it.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Turn on Discreet notifications in the app (Settings → Notifications) to hide cycle details from your lock screen.

No method of storing data electronically is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information and only keep what the Service needs.

4. Services We Use

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and backup. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and Apple Health. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google — Google Sign-In, and Google Maps for the Safe Space map and nearby places. Google Privacy Policy
  • Vercel and Firebase Hosting — host our websites.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

5. This Website

If you join our waitlist, we store your first name and email address to tell you when Align'd launches. Email us to be removed at any time. This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when you visit.

6. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • On your phone: kept until you delete it in the app or delete the app.
  • Your account: in the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account and your profile, cycle and health data from our servers straight away. Copies in our database provider's backups are removed within 30 days.
  • Community posts and orders: after you delete your account these are no longer linked to you. Community posts stay visible under their anonymous handle, so delete any you want removed before deleting your account, or email us. Order records are kept as long as we need them for deliveries and accounting.
  • Waitlist: kept until launch or until you ask us to remove you.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Restrict or object to how we use your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time — for example by disconnecting Apple Health or deleting your account

To use any of these rights, email support@aligned-wellness.com. We will reply within 30 days.

8. European and UK Users (GDPR)

  • We process your health data only with your explicit consent, which you give when you set up the app. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Health data is special category data under Article 9 of the GDPR, and we treat it that way.
  • If your data is transferred outside your country, we rely on our providers' standard contractual safeguards.
  • You have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. California Users (CCPA)

  • You have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, and to ask us to delete it.
  • You have the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • We will not treat you differently for using your privacy rights.

10. Age

Align'd Wellness is rated 18+ on the App Store and is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email support@aligned-wellness.com and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes, we will update this page and the policy in the app, and change the "Last Updated" date above.
